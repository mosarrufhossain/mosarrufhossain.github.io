Research

Research interests

Atmospheric wind and wake field estimation using small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS); stochastic and model-based state estimation (EKF, EnKF); flight dynamics and aircraft system identification; UAS-based sensing of wildfire-induced flow, aircraft wake vortices, and other turbulent environments including urban canyons; observability analysis and distributed, multi-vehicle sensing architectures for wind-field mapping.
