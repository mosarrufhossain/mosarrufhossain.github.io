Research Interests

🌪️

Wind & Gust Sensing

Developing methods to sense and estimate atmospheric wind fields, gusts, and turbulence using onboard UAS sensors and state estimation algorithms.

🔥

Fire-Induced Wind Estimation

Investigating fire plume dynamics and estimating fire-induced vertical wind velocities during UAS fire plume encounters using stochastic filters.

✈️

Wake Vortex Research

Formulating optimal wake-parameter identification methods and extending flight-dynamics simulations for UAS response to wake vortices.

🛰️

UAS System Identification

Identifying servo, actuator, and full-system dynamics for small and micro UAS using open-loop and closed-loop flight data to support control design.

🔬

Small/Micro UAS Development

Designing and testing small fixed-wing and flying-wing UAS platforms, including end-to-end flight test campaigns with full safety review protocols.

📊

Stochastic Filtering

Building and validating probabilistic estimation frameworks for real-time inference of flow angles and atmospheric states during UAS flight.
